Made by game developers.
For game developers.

Claudette knows the engines, editors and depots games are made with. Open a project's folder, and its tab knows what's in it: the engine and its version, how to build and launch it, and where the editor writes its log.

01 Tabs and sessions

A dozen sessions.
Not one lost.

Every session gets a tab, grouped by project in a sidebar that shows each one's status, branch, model and context at a glance. Mark tabs your way, and press Ctrl/Cmd+J for the next one waiting for you. Fold a busy tab's sub-threads, subagents and logs away under it.

02 Plans, tasks and threads

Big jobs.
Shared out.

Follow Claude's plan as it writes it, with every version, and watch each task get done: who's on it, how long it took and what it changed. Make a tab a thread, and its Claude hands work to the other tabs in its project, with one report back.

04 Usage and limits

Know your plan.
Before it runs out.

Live meters for the session and the week, a projection of where you'll land, and alerts before you hit a limit. If you do hit one mid-task, the tab waits for the reset and carries on by itself.

Every change.
Reviewed.

The code,

07 Editor logs

The editor's log.
Right under the tab.

While Unreal, Unity or Godot has the project open, its log is a row under the tab, followed as it's written, with its errors and warnings counted. Filter it by level, category or text, click a file it names to open it, and when the editor crashes, the log stays to say why.

10 Settings

Settings that say
what they do.

Each setting has a line saying what it does, and the search finds it by what you'd call it. Changes apply as you make them, and Appearance shows them in a preview. Settings stays open beside your work, so you can see what a change does, and each section resets on its own.

The Settings window {% include icon.html name="arrow-right" %}

Make it yours

Two looks.
Light and dark.

Standard is dense and calm, in your OS's accent color. Claude takes after the Claude apps, with warm colors, rounder cards and a serif for replies. Both follow your system, or not.

Styles and themes {% include icon.html name="arrow-right" %}

Meet Claudette

She keeps you
company.

Claudette stands on the message box while you work. She reads along with a magnifying glass, hammers while Claude edits, juggles a ball for each subagent, waves when Claude needs you, and throws confetti when the task list is done.

Poke her and drag her about, and play her at tic-tac-toe while a usage limit resets. She stands still if you reduce motion, and Settings can turn her off.
