# Settings

Claudette's settings are in one window, with a page for each part of the app. This chapter covers the window itself, a table of every setting with its default and where it's explained, the two looks Claudette has and what it does for people who need less motion or a screen reader, how to change its shortcuts, the Advanced page, and About Claudette, with the trademarks Claudette names and the licenses of what it's built with.

Each setting is explained on the page about the part it changes. [Every setting](every-setting.md) is the way in from the other direction: find the setting, then follow its link.
