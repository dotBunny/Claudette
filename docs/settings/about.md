# About

{% include unreleased.html %}

Settings → About says which Claudette you have and where to find it on the web, whose names and logos it uses, and the open-source libraries, font and artwork it's built with, each with its license. It has no settings.

{% include shot.html name="guide/settings/about" alt="Settings on About: the version, On the web with Website, Source code, Changelog and Privacy policy, Claudette's MIT License, and the Trademarks section with a row for each name and its owner's notice" %}

## Opening it

- **About**, the last page of the Claudette group in Settings' sidebar.
- **About Claudette** in the menu at the header's right.
- **About Claudette** in **Help**, the question mark at the foot of Settings' sidebar.
- On macOS, **About Claudette** in the app menu.
- **About** in the [command palette](../tabs/the-command-palette.md) (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+P`).

## Claudette

- **The version**, the same as at the foot of Settings' sidebar, where a click copies the versions for a bug report ([The version and Help](the-settings-window.md#the-version-and-help)).
- **On the web**: **Website**, **Source code**, **Changelog** and **Privacy policy** open in your browser.
- **MIT License**: Claudette is free and open source, made by dotBunny. **License** shows the license's text under it.

## Trademarks

Claudette names Claude Code, and the engines and tools it works with, to say what it works with. Each has a row with its owner's notice: Claude and Claude Code, Godot and the Godot logo, Unity, Unreal, Unreal Engine and the Unreal Engine logo, and Perforce, P4V and the Perforce logo. Claudette is made by dotBunny, and isn't sponsored by, affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

Godot's logo is the icon of Godot projects, on their page in Settings and on the [project's row](../project-tools/using-project-tools.md) at the foot of the sidebar, and its whole logo, in color, is the title of Settings → [Godot](../project-tools/godot.md). Claudette uses it under the Godot Foundation's trademark policy, and the logo itself is by Andrea Calabró, under CC BY 4.0. Unity has an icon Claudette draws for it.

Unreal Engine's logo is the icon of Unreal Engine projects, on their page in Settings and on the project's row, and the title of Settings → [Unreal Engine](../project-tools/unreal-engine.md). Claudette shows it with Epic's permission, in Epic's black, or its white in a dark theme.

Perforce's logo is on Settings → [Perforce](../project-tools/perforce.md): its symbol beside the page's name in the sidebar, and the whole logo as the page's title. Claudette shows it with Perforce's permission, only in Perforce's own colors, whatever the style: in the sidebar, black (white in a dark theme) until you select the page.

## Open-source licenses

The last section lists everything Claudette is built with that someone else made: each library it ships, the .NET runtime, Inter (the interface's font) and Godot's logo. Each row has the name and version, the license and whose it is.

- **Website** opens its project's page.
- **License** shows the license's text under the row. For a license that asks only for a link, such as the logo's CC BY 4.0, it opens the license's page instead.
- **Notices**, on some rows, shows the third-party notices it carries: the licenses of what it's built from in turn.

The list is made from the libraries the copy you have was built with, so it matches your version.
